Grey Structure Cost Per Square Foot in Lahore: What the Rate Covers

What a grey structure rate per sq ft in Lahore actually buys, how to compare quotes fairly, and how to turn a rate into a budget for your own plot.

💬 Book a Free Site Visit 📞 Call 0306-1982828

Overview

A grey structure rate looks like a simple number, and that is the trap. Two contractors can quote the same figure per square foot and deliver very different houses, or quote different figures and mean the same thing. The number only means something once you know what sits behind it.

This guide explains what a grey structure rate covers, how to compare rates fairly and how to turn one into a budget.

What Grey Structure Includes

Our grey structure scope covers the foundation, RCC frame, block or brick masonry, roof slabs, plaster and conduit placement. It is the shell of the house, ready for finishing. It does not include tiling, woodwork, paint or sanitary fixtures, which belong to the turnkey scopes.

As of September 29, 2026, our grey structure rate is PKR 2,500 to 3,000 per square foot of covered area. It is indicative, and your final figure is confirmed after a free site visit. The pricing page shows it beside our labour-only and turnkey rates.

Why Quotes for the Same Rate Differ

Check three things. First, material grades and brands: cement, steel and brick quality change cost and strength, and they should be named in writing. Second, what is counted in the area: some quotes count open space or boundary walls differently. Third, what is left out, such as excavation, plaster or conduit. A cheaper rate that leaves items out is not cheaper.

Questions to Ask Every Contractor

Ask for the specification in writing, the payment schedule tied to stages and the validity period of the quote. Ask who inspects each stage. Our grey structure mistakes to avoid explains what goes wrong when these are skipped, and our grey structure page sets out our own specification.

Reading a Quote Line by Line

A proper grey structure quote lists its parts: excavation, foundation and footings, columns and beams, brick or block masonry, roof slabs, plaster, the damp proof course and conduit placement. It also states the steel and cement grades, how curing will be handled and what formwork is included. If a quote is a single lump sum with no breakdown, ask for one. A breakdown lets you see exactly what a lower figure leaves out, and it gives you something to hold the contractor to later.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the grey structure cost per square foot in Lahore?

Our grey structure rate as of September 29, 2026 is PKR 2,500 to 3,000 per square foot of covered area. It is indicative, and your exact figure is confirmed after a free site visit.

What does a grey structure rate include?

The foundation, RCC frame, block or brick masonry, roof slabs, plaster and conduit placement. Tiling, woodwork, paint and sanitary fixtures are not part of grey structure.

How do I calculate the grey structure cost for my house?

Multiply the rate by your covered area, measured slab to slab. For example, 2,000 sq ft at PKR 2,500 to 3,000 is roughly PKR 50 to 60 lakh.

Why do two grey structure quotes at the same rate differ?

The material brands and grades, what is counted in the area, and which items are left out can all differ. Compare quotes on the same written specification.

Is a lower grey structure rate always cheaper?

No. A lower rate that leaves out items or uses weaker materials can cost more in the end. Compare the full specification, not just the figure.
