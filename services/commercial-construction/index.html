Commercial Construction That Opens On Time

Commercial projects run on a different clock than residential ones. Every week of delay can mean lost rent or a delayed business opening. We scope commercial jobs around your actual deadline, using the same A+ grade materials and structural standards as our residential work, with a fixed quote agreed before the first brick is laid.

Why Commercial Construction Needs a Different Approach Than a House

A house has one owner and one use case decided upfront. A commercial building often has to accommodate a use case that isn't fully finalized when construction starts, a tenant's specific electrical load, a retail layout that might change before opening, or a plaza's multiple future tenants with different fit-out needs. That uncertainty is exactly why we don't sell commercial construction off a fixed price list the way some contractors advertise; it invites corner-cutting on the parts of the scope that are genuinely variable.

The structural fundamentals don't change, though. Commercial buildings use the same grade of cement, steel, and structural practice as our residential grey structure work, sized to the specific load the building needs to carry. A retail unit and an office floor above it don't carry the same load, and we size the structure to what's actually being built, not a one-size-fits-all commercial default.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you build offices and commercial plazas, not just houses?

Yes. Commercial Construction is one of our three core services alongside Gray Structure and House Construction: offices, plazas, and other commercial spaces.

How is commercial construction priced differently from a house?

Commercial projects vary much more in scope than residential plots, so pricing is based on a site visit and your specific drawings rather than a fixed per-marla rate. You still get a fixed, itemized quote within 24 hours, locked for 30 days.

Can you work around a business's opening deadline?

Yes, deadline-driven scheduling is discussed at the site visit stage so the build plan is set around your target opening date from day one.

What areas do you cover for commercial projects?

Lahore, Pakistan, with particular experience in Bahria Town and Lake City, alongside projects across the rest of the city.

Do you handle multi-tenant plazas with different fit-out needs?

Yes. We plan the structural scope to accommodate multiple future tenants, shared load-bearing considerations, and different fit-out timelines as part of the initial site visit and quote.

What materials do you use for commercial structural work?

The same A+ grade lineup as our residential grey structure: Maple Leaf Cement, Mughal Supreme Steel 60 Grade, and semi special bricks, sized to your specific project's load requirements rather than a fixed residential spec.
