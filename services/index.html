Three Decades of Building in Lahore

MS Construction Services has been building in Lahore since 1994. That's long enough to have worked through several cycles of material price swings, changing society bylaws, and shifting standards across Bahria Town, Lake City, DHA, and the rest of the city, and to have learned which shortcuts cost clients money later and which ones genuinely don't matter.

We deliberately limit how many house construction projects we take on each month (three, at most) so that site supervision stays personal rather than stretched across too many jobs at once. The same standards apply whether the project is a single grey structure, a full turnkey house, or a commercial fit-out.

How to Choose the Right Service

You already have an architect and drawings, and just need the structure built

Choose Grey Structure, the foundation, columns, beams, slabs, brickwork, and roof, built to your approved drawings.

You want a complete home, from foundation to move-in ready, managed by one team

Choose House Construction, grey structure through finishing and furnishing under one fixed quote.

You're building a retail unit, office, or plaza

Choose Commercial Construction, scoped around your business's opening deadline.

You need drawings before you can even get a quote

Start with Architectural Design, floor plans, elevation design, and structural drawings, then move into construction once they're approved.

You already have a house and need work done on it

Home Renovation for structural repairs and additions, or Interior Finishing if the structure's fine and you just need the inside redone.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Which service is right for me?

If you already have a structure and need it built, choose Grey Structure. If you need a complete house from foundation to move-in ready, choose House Construction. For offices, plazas, or other commercial spaces, choose Commercial Construction. We also offer home renovation, architectural design, interior finishing, and project management as standalone services.

Do all services include a free site visit?

Yes. Every service starts with a free site visit and a fixed, itemized quote within 24 hours, locked for 30 days with no hidden costs.

What areas do you serve?

Lahore, Pakistan, with a strong track record in Bahria Town (Sector A, C, and E) and Lake City.

How long have you been in business?

Since 1994. See our completed projects and case studies for real examples of recent work.

Can I combine services, like architectural design and construction?

Yes. It's common for a project to start with Architectural Design and move directly into Grey Structure or House Construction once drawings are approved, all under the same team and point of contact.

Do your services come with a warranty?

Yes. MS Construction Services provides a 10-year warranty on our construction work. Full warranty terms are confirmed in writing as part of your project agreement.
