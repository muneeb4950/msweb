Overseas Pakistani Construction in Lahore, Done Without Being on Site

Building a house in Lahore while you live in another country is very common, and it is also where projects most often go wrong. The distance is not the real problem. The problem is that nobody you trust is standing on the site when a decision is made, a shortcut is taken or a payment is asked for. Good overseas construction is a system for closing that gap.

We regularly manage projects for clients abroad who cannot be on site day to day. The way we do it is simple and written down: updates over WhatsApp with photos and videos at each stage, every quote and milestone kept in writing, and a site covered by 24/7 CCTV. This page explains what that looks like and what you should prepare before you start.

What You See From Abroad

You do not need to fly in to know how the house is going. At each stage you receive photos and videos over WhatsApp, and the milestone just completed is recorded in writing before the next one begins. Stages usually run from excavation and foundation, through columns, beams and the roof slab, to brickwork, plaster and finishing. Because the site is also watched by CCTV around the clock, the record does not rest on anyone's memory or goodwill.

One client, an overseas Pakistani who built a 5 Marla house in Bahria Town, wrote in his Google review that our care and communication meant a lot to him. That kind of communication is exactly what this service is built around.

Before You Start: What to Prepare From Abroad

A little preparation saves weeks. Have your plot documents and society details ready, including the plot number, block and size. Settle your drawings before construction, not during it, because design changes are the hardest thing to manage across time zones. Our architectural design service can prepare floor plans and structural drawings for approval in your society.

Decide who can act for you in Pakistan. Many overseas owners nominate a relative or trusted friend to attend the site visit and answer quick questions. If that person will sign or approve anything for you, speak to a lawyer about the right documents first. It also helps to decide early which choices stay with you alone, such as design changes and the budget.

Money, Quotes and Staying in Control

Ask every builder how payments are tied to progress. With us the quote is fixed and itemized, and the price stays locked for 30 days, so you know the figure you are deciding on. Our pricing page shows the rate per square foot for labour-only, grey structure and turnkey scopes, and explains that we measure slab to slab, so open areas are not charged. Agree in writing what each milestone covers before it falls due, and keep the paperwork in one folder you can open from anywhere.

If you would rather keep your own builder but want someone independent on site, our project management service provides supervision and milestone checks on a project we did not build. Our guide to building on a fixed budget is a good companion read.

Grey Structure First, or Turnkey?

Owners abroad usually choose between two routes. The first is the grey structure now, with finishing later when a trip home is possible, which spreads the cost and lets you choose tiles and fittings in person. The second is turnkey design and build, where one team draws, builds and finishes the house so you receive it ready to move into.

Either way, choose a builder you can check. Our guide on finding a reliable contractor in Lahore lists what to ask, and you can see how we work on our construction company in Lahore page. We build across many societies, listed on the areas we serve hub.
