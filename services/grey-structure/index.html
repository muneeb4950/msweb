What Grey Structure Construction Covers

Grey structure is the structural skeleton of your home or commercial building: excavation, foundation, DPC, RCC columns and beams, floor slabs, brickwork, and roof structure. Get this stage right, and everything built on top of it (finishing, electrical, plumbing) goes smoothly. Get it wrong, and problems show up years later as cracks, dampness, or structural issues that are expensive to fix.

MS Construction Services has been building grey structures in Lahore since 1994, with a strong recent track record in Bahria Town (Sector A, Sector C, and Sector E) and Lake City. Several of our Google reviews mention grey structure completed in as little as 100 days.

Why Grey Structure Quality Matters More Than Any Other Stage

Every other stage of a build, plaster, paint, tiling, even electrical, can be redone without touching the bones of the house. Grey structure can't. Once the foundation is poured and the RCC columns, beams, and slabs are cast, that structural decision is locked in for the life of the building. A weak DPC shows up as rising damp five years later. Undersized steel shows up as hairline cracks that widen over time. So we treat grey structure as the stage that determines everything downstream, not just another line item.

This is also why we don't compete on the lowest grey structure price. A lower quote almost always means a compromise somewhere in the mix, thinner steel, mixed sand instead of pure Chenab or Ravi sand, or skipped curing time, and none of those show up in a walkthrough the week construction finishes. They show up later, in a client's maintenance budget, not ours.

What "A+ Grade" Actually Means, Spec by Spec

The specific standards behind each material choice, not just brand names.

Steel: Mughal Supreme 60 Grade

Grade 60 steel has a minimum yield strength of 60,000 psi, the standard specification for residential and light commercial RCC work in Pakistan. We size bar diameter and spacing to your structural drawings rather than using a single default across every plot, since a 5 Marla house and a 1 Kanal house don't carry the same load.

Cement: Maple Leaf Ordinary Portland

We mix to the ratios called for in your structural drawings (commonly 1:2:4 for footings, richer mixes for slabs and columns), and we don't shortcut curing time. Concrete that's under-cured loses a meaningful share of its design strength, even if the mix itself was correct.

Sand & Aggregate: Not Mixed, Not Substituted

Chenab and Ravi sand kept separate (not blended with cheaper local sand), Sargodha plant crush, and bajri rather than rori. These choices affect concrete workability and long-term strength more than most homeowners realize, and they're also the easiest place for a low-cost quote to quietly cut corners.

DPC & Waterproofing

Damp-proof course is installed at plinth level on every project, not treated as optional. It's a small line item that's genuinely cheap to get right during construction and genuinely expensive to fix after the walls and finishing are done.

Common Grey Structure Mistakes We See (and Avoid)

Skipping or shortening curing time is the single most common shortcut in Lahore's construction market. Concrete needs consistent moisture for at least 7 days to develop meaningful strength and closer to 28 days for full design strength. Rushing this to hit a deadline is invisible on handover day and expensive years later.

Misaligned floors are the second. When upper-floor walls don't sit directly above ground-floor walls or columns, the load path isn't straight down anymore, it requires additional beams and transfer structures to carry it safely. We check this against the approved drawings before brickwork starts, not after.

Undersized footings on weak or variable soil is the third, and it's specific to certain pockets of Lahore and parts of Bahria Town and Lake City where soil conditions aren't uniform. We don't apply a single default foundation depth across every plot; we adjust based on what the site actually is.

What's included in your grey structure package?

Excavation, foundation, DPC, RCC columns, beams and slabs, brickwork, and roof structure, built using Maple Leaf Cement, Mughal Supreme Steel 60 Grade, semi special bricks, and the other materials listed above. A written scope is provided with your fixed quote.

How long does grey structure construction take?

Grey structure alone is typically the first 6-8 weeks of that timeline for a 5-10 Marla plot, though a 10 Marla grey-structure-only project we completed in Bahria Town BB Block took 100 days start to finish.

Do you provide site supervision during grey structure?

Yes. Every site has 24/7 CCTV camera surveillance, and we personally check structural milestones, foundation depth, steel placement, slab thickness, before moving to the next stage.

Do you build grey structure in Bahria Town and Lake City?

Yes. Grey structure is one of our most requested services in Bahria Town (Sector A, C, and E) and Lake City, and we also take on projects across the rest of Lahore.

What steel grade do you use for grey structure?

Mughal Supreme Steel 60 Grade, sized to your specific structural drawings. We don't use a single default bar spacing across every plot size.

How do you prevent cracks and dampness in grey structure?

Correct DPC installation at plinth level, full concrete curing time rather than rushing it, and checking that upper floors align with the structure below rather than requiring makeshift transfer beams. These are the three most common causes of later structural problems, and all three are addressed during construction, not after.
