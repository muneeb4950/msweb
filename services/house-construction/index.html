What Actually Moves House Construction Cost

We don't publish a flat per-marla rate for full house construction, because the honest answer is that it depends on more than plot size. Finishing grade is the biggest swing factor: standard tiling and fittings versus imported finishes can change a budget by a meaningful margin on the same structural footprint.

Design complexity matters almost as much. A straightforward rectangular layout with aligned floors costs less to build than one with cantilevered balconies, large open spans, or a basement, because those choices need extra steel, concrete, and formwork regardless of finishing grade. Soil conditions play a role too: most Lake City and Bahria Town plots we've built on have consistent, predictable soil, but a plot with weak or variable soil needs a stronger foundation, which adds cost before the structure is even visible above ground.

This is exactly why we quote after a site visit and a look at your drawings, rather than off a generic rate card. It's slower than publishing a number on a page, but it means the quote you get is the number you actually pay.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to build a house in Lahore?

It depends on plot size: a 5 Marla house typically takes 2-3 months, a 10 Marla house 3-4 months, a 1 Kanal house 4-5 months, and a 2 Kanal house 6-8 months for complete construction.

How much does house construction cost per marla in Lahore?

Costs vary based on plot size, finishing grade, design complexity, and site conditions, so we don't quote a flat per-marla rate. We provide a free site visit and a fixed, itemized quote within 24 hours, locked for 30 days with no hidden costs.

Do you build houses in Bahria Town and Lake City?

Yes. Several of our completed houses are in Bahria Town (Sector A, C, and E) and Lake City, and we build across the rest of Lahore as well.

Can I hire you just for grey structure, not the full house?

Yes. See our Grey Structure page: grey structure and full house construction are both available as separate fixed-quote services.

Do you manage the finishing and furnishing stages too, or just structure?

Full house construction covers all three stages, grey structure, finishing, and furnishing, under one fixed quote. If you only want grey structure, that's available separately too.

Why do you only take 3 new house construction projects a month?

So each client's project gets full attention from site visit to handover rather than being one of a dozen simultaneous builds. It also means our 24/7 CCTV site supervision is consistent rather than stretched thin.

How much does grey structure cost per square foot within a house construction project?

As of September 25, 2026, our grey structure construction cost is approximately Rs 2,850 per square foot in Lahore. Full turnkey construction, including finishing, is quoted separately based on your chosen materials and finish level, confirmed in a fixed, itemized quote after the free site visit.

Does house construction come with a warranty?

Yes. MS Construction Services provides a 10-year warranty on our construction work. Full warranty terms are confirmed in writing as part of your project agreement.
