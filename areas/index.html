Why We Have a Page for Every Society

Nearly all of the building we do in Lahore happens inside housing societies rather than on open city streets. Every society runs by its own rules: who approves your drawings, which hours a truck of bricks may pass the gate, how a boundary wall is shared with the neighbour. The structure of a house is the same wherever it stands. The paperwork, the access and the calendar are what change from one society to the next.

So each society and sector below has its own page, covering what to plan for there and answering the questions owners in that area ask us most. If your plot is on the list, start with its page. If it is not, message us anyway, because we also build elsewhere in Lahore.

How the Societies Differ, and What We Plan For

Gated societies with their own approval process

Bahria Town Lahore and Bahria Orchard Lahore run controlled gate entry, set hours for material delivery and society-level plan approval. DHA Lahore and EME Society Lahore, now managed as DHA's EME Sector, work through DHA's building control, where structural drawings are approved before work starts and inspections happen at defined stages. We put those steps into your project timeline on the first day, so they do not appear as surprises in month three.

The Raiwind Road side of the city

Lake City Lahore is the society behind the Google reviews quoted on our pages, and Lake City Sector M-3 has its own page for the larger plots there. Al-Kabir Town Phase 2 sits on Main Raiwind Road directly opposite Lake City, AWT Housing Scheme is a walled, guarded society on the same road, and Fazaia Housing Scheme is a PAF-developed community with two phases. Al-Kabir Town Lahore covers Phase 1 near Beaconhouse National University, which is a different location from Phase 2.

Newer schemes and established neighbourhoods

Etihad Town Lahore is still developing, so many plots there are being built on for the first time and utility access can vary by block. Jubilee Town Lahore sits off Multan Road, where compact plots make vertical planning and shared boundary walls the main design questions. Central Park Housing Scheme lies on Ferozepur Road near the Ring Road, which usually helps with material sourcing. Bankers Avenue Society is the opposite case: an established cooperative society where new houses go up beside occupied ones, so neighbour coordination and shared walls need extra care.

The Same Process in Every Area

Wherever your plot is, the sequence does not change. We visit the site for free and check access, levels and utilities. Within 24 hours you receive a fixed, itemized quote, and the price stays locked for 30 days so you can decide without pressure. Once you go ahead, construction uses the materials named in your quote, and we coordinate the society side of things, from drawing approvals to gate passes, alongside the build itself.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Which areas of Lahore does MS Construction Services build in?

We take grey structure and house construction projects in Bahria Town, Bahria Orchard, DHA Lahore, EME Society, Lake City (including Sector M-3), Al-Kabir Town Phase 1 and Phase 2, AWT Housing Scheme, Fazaia Housing Scheme, Etihad Town, Jubilee Town, Central Park Housing Scheme and Bankers Avenue Society.

My society is not on your list. Can you still build there?

Possibly. Our work is centred on the societies above, but we also build elsewhere in Lahore. Send the society name and plot size on WhatsApp at 0306-1982828 and we will tell you honestly whether we can take it on.

Do society rules really change how long a house takes to build?

They can. Gated societies control gate entry and the hours materials may be delivered, and DHA requires structural drawings to be approved before work begins, with inspections at set stages. We put these steps into your timeline at the start so they do not turn into delays halfway through.

Is the quote different for each area?

The structural specification is the same everywhere. What differs is the plot, the design and the site conditions, so we confirm everything at a free site visit and then send a fixed, itemized quote within 24 hours, locked for 30 days.

Al-Kabir Town has two pages. Which one is mine?

They are two separate locations. Phase 1 is near Beaconhouse National University, while Phase 2 is on Main Raiwind Road directly opposite Lake City. Check where your plot sits and open the matching page.

Is the site visit free in every area?

Yes. Book through WhatsApp or call 0306-1982828, and we will visit the plot, check access and utilities, and talk through your plans before any quote is issued.
