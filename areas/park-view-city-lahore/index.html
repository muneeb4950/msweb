Building in Park View City

Park View City in Lahore is a large private scheme with a modern layout, and owners are at every stage from a bare plot to a finished home. We take on grey structure and house construction in Park View City, and the free site visit begins with a question that decides a lot of the cost: what is actually connected to your street today?

Park View City: Location and Blocks

Park View City is on Multan Road, about 3 km from the Thokar Niaz Baig junction and opposite DHA EME Sector. It covers around 7,000 Kanal and has many named blocks, including Crystal, Diamond, Executive, Gold, Jade, Platinum and others, with plot sizes from about 5 Marla to 1 Kanal depending on the block.

Because block names and plot sizes differ across listings, we always match your plot file and map before drawing. During the free visit we check access from Multan Road for material trucks and plan deliveries accordingly. See our house construction service for details.

Ask the Developer in Writing

Ask the developer or the society office to confirm your plot's handover status, the services available and the construction rules that apply. Written answers are worth keeping.

Possession Is Not the Same as Readiness

Having a plot allotted or handed over does not mean it is ready for a builder. Check that the plot is physically marked, that the road in front is open to loaded trucks and that water, sewer and electricity are either connected or have a firm date. Each missing item adds temporary arrangements to the build, and each has a cost. We list what we find at the visit and what we have assumed in the quote.

Choosing the Right Time to Start

Starting too early can cost money if the street is not ready: trucks get stuck, water is trucked in and work stops. Starting too late can cost too if prices rise in the meantime. Our quote holds for 30 days, so you can decide without pressure, and the pricing page shows current rates by scope.

If you want the shell built first and the finishing done later, the grey structure service covers what is included. For a single agreement from drawings to move-in, read about turnkey design and build.

What We Look at on Your Street

At the site visit we check the road, the nearest water and power connections and how far the neighbours have got. A plot you can walk onto may still be weeks from being ready for trucks. We write down what we found and let it shape both the schedule and the price.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Is my Park View City plot ready to build on once I get possession?

Not necessarily. Check the road, water, power and the developer's building rules, then book a visit so we can confirm.

What should I ask the developer before starting?

Construction rules, plan approval steps, fees, delivery restrictions and when your street's services are due.

What should I check before building in Park View City?

Check that the plot is marked and handed over, the road is open to trucks and water, sewer and power are connected or scheduled. We record what we find at the visit.

Does the season matter for starting a foundation?

Yes. Excavation and concrete go better in dry weather, so plan the foundation away from heavy rain.

How do I tell if my street is ready for trucks?

Look at the road surface and width, and ask neighbours how deliveries have gone. We also check at the visit.

Can I start with just a boundary wall?

Yes. Many owners secure the plot first. It is priced separately from the house.
