Grey Structure Construction

The grey structure forms the base for everything that follows. It is also the stage where clear drawings and specifications matter most.

Our grey structure service covers the agreed structural scope, including excavation, foundations, damp-proof course, RCC work, slabs and brick masonry. Ask for a written schedule identifying the materials, quantities and work included in your quotation.

Before Building in Etihad Town, Check Your Specific Plot

Your phase and block are useful starting points, but the assessment needs to reach the plot itself. Before setting a construction start date, establish:

✓

Whether possession and permission to start construction are in place.

✓

Which building requirements and drawing approvals apply to the property.

✓

Whether the drawings match the plot dimensions and proposed use.

✓

What arrangements are needed for construction water, power and deliveries.

✓

Whether the site level or ground conditions require further assessment.

If your plot sits below road level, flag it during the site visit. Filling, floor levels and drainage need professional assessment before a final scope is agreed.

Send your phase, block, plot size and location pin so the discussion starts with your property’s actual requirements.

How Much Does House Construction Cost in Etihad Town Lahore?

A useful construction estimate connects the price to a defined scope. The main inputs include covered area, number of floors, structural requirements, site conditions and finishing choices.

Before comparing quotations, compare these details:

What to compareWhat to ask
MeasurementWhich areas are measured, and how?
MaterialsWhich brands, grades and specifications are included?
ScopeWhat work is included, excluded or priced separately?
FinishingWhich products or allowances does the price cover?
PaymentsWhat work or milestone triggers each payment?
ChangesHow will additions and substitutions be approved and priced?

A lower headline rate can cover a smaller scope. Ask for the differences in writing before deciding.

Can I hire you for grey structure only?

Can I get an estimate before my drawings are complete?

You can begin a budget discussion using your plot dimensions, intended floors and requirements. A detailed quotation needs a sufficiently defined design and scope; an early estimate should be treated accordingly.

The label alone does not tell you what you are buying. Ask for the actual material brands, grades, specifications and workmanship requirements. MS Construction Services can discuss its material specification sheet with you.

How long will my house take to build?
