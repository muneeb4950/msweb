Grey Structure Construction

Grey structure construction covers the main structural and base works before finishing. Depending on the written scope, this may include site preparation, excavation, foundations, steel reinforcement, concrete, brick masonry, roof slabs, plaster, water tanks, plumbing lines and electrical conduits. Package inclusions can differ, so we encourage you to check the actual work listed in a quotation rather than relying only on the words 'grey structure'. Full material specifications are listed on our grey structure page.

Because the grey structure is later covered by finishes, it deserves careful attention while the work is visible. Clients should understand what is happening at this stage and why. We guide clients through the construction sequence and discuss important material and workmanship decisions in a clear way.

A strong structure cannot be judged only from a finished wall or fresh paint. Correct planning, appropriate reinforcement, well-managed concrete work, careful brickwork and coordinated service lines all contribute to the long-term performance of the building.

Complete Turnkey House Construction

For clients who prefer one company to manage the complete build, MS Construction Services offers turnkey house construction. A turnkey project can cover the grey structure as well as finishing work such as flooring, ceilings, paint, doors, wardrobes, kitchens, sanitary fittings, electrical fixtures, exterior finishes and other agreed details.

Turnkey construction is helpful when a client wants one coordinated team rather than having to manage multiple contractors. It can also make decision-making more structured because the design, material selections and site work can be discussed as part of one overall plan.

The exact package should always be agreed in writing, including the scope, material brands or specifications where applicable, exclusions, estimated schedule and payment milestones. Clear scope planning is important for both the client and the contractor.

House Renovation and Remodeling

Our renovation services are designed for practical improvements as well as visual upgrades. We can help plan internal changes, finishing improvements, paint work, ceiling work, woodwork, wardrobes, kitchen upgrades, bathroom renovation, electrical updates and other required improvements.

Renovation requires careful planning because work often has to be completed around an existing structure, live utility lines or occupied rooms. We discuss the required changes before work begins so the client understands the likely sequence and impact of the renovation.

Design & Finishing

Architectural Design, Planning and Interior Finishing

A good construction project starts with a plan that reflects the client's needs. We provide architectural design and planning support so that clients can think through the house or commercial building before physical construction begins.

The planning stage is the best time to make changes. It is far easier and more cost-effective to improve a layout on paper than to break walls after construction has started. We encourage clients to take time at this stage and consider their current requirements as well as future needs.

Architectural Design and Space Planning

Architectural planning is not limited to a front elevation. It includes room sizes, movement between spaces, ventilation, sunlight, privacy, storage, parking and the relationship between indoor and outdoor areas. We help clients choose a plan that suits the plot and family lifestyle. See our architectural design service for more.

For residential projects, we discuss the number of rooms, kitchen requirements, stairs, lounges, guest areas, bathrooms, terraces and service spaces. For commercial projects, we consider access, working areas, client-facing spaces, storage and future flexibility.

Interior Finishing Services

Interior finishing gives the building its final character. It can include flooring, wall finishes, paint, ceilings, lighting coordination, doors, woodwork, wardrobes, kitchens, sanitary fittings, stairs, railings and exterior finishing. See our interior finishing service for more.

A photograph can help explain the look you like, but there are other questions to ask before choosing a finish. Is the floor easy to clean? Is the kitchen storage enough? Will a replacement fitting be easy to find? We discuss appearance alongside everyday use and maintenance. This helps you choose finishes you will be comfortable living with, rather than making every decision from a catalogue picture.

Jo design tasveer mein pasand aaye, zaroor share karein. Phir dekhte hain ke woh aap ke room ke size, budget aur rozmarrah istemal ke liye kitna munasib hai.
